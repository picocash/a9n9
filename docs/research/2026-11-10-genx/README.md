# Gen X portal research, 2026-11-10

Three research reports behind the "Gen X global plan" (Claude Doc "Gen X portal plan", tab "Global plan":
https://claude.ai/artifact/MEcSBhrCWifqBXobpyekbh#52b9a341-975a).

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `01-media-and-newsletters.md` | Existing Gen X and midlife newsletters, podcasts, creators; who has paying subscribers |
| `02-communities-and-memberships.md` | Communities and memberships for this age group, what lasted, what failed and why, fraud and safety practice |
| `03-global-and-cross-border.md` | Where the "Gen X" label is understood, cohort sizes by country, cross-border networks, shared themes |

Each fact is marked verified or reported, with its source URL. The reports were written by research
subagents on 2026-11-10; their web-search budgets ran out before every thread was closed, so gaps are
named in each file.

Not researched: GDPR, trademarks for any candidate name, willingness to pay outside the US and UK.
