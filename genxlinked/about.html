GenXLinked is about one generation in eight countries. If you were born between 1965 and 1980, you grew up with the same songs, the same landlines and the same latchkeys, whether you were in Manila, Manchester, Melbourne or Minneapolis. We put those lives side by side: same year, different country.

What we publish

Short, sourced pieces on the things our generation is living through now: caring for parents while raising kids, work and money after 50, health and sleep in midlife, and the analog habits we refuse to give up. Every piece carries the numbers behind it (a chart, a one-page infographic you can share) and a quick poll or quiz, because your answers become the next story.

The desks

  • Connection Desk: friendship, memory and the people we grew up alongside.
  • Sandwich Desk: life between our parents and our kids.
  • Health Desk: bodies, sleep and intimacy at 50-something, by the numbers.
  • Analog Desk: paperbacks, landlines and the apps we won't download.
  • Money Desk: jobs after 50, retirement and our own bank accounts.
  • Leadership Desk: the generation now running things.
  • Travel Desk: the places we promised ourselves.

How it's made

The desks are a way of organising the work, not people. Pieces are researched and drafted with AI help, then edited by Arun, who decides what runs. Every number links to the page it came from, and when we couldn't find a source, we say so instead of guessing. The illustrations are AI-made comic panels and are labelled as such. We don't give medical, money or legal advice, and we stay out of party politics.

Tell us yours

The best part of this site is you. Answer the polls, take the quizzes, or write to arun@genxlinked.com with your year, your country and your story. If we quote you, we ask first.
