Short version: we collect as little as we can, we don't sell it, and we don't run ads or ad trackers.

What we collect

  • If you join: your email address and, if you give it, your first name. We use them to sign you in and to send the weekly letter. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address and first name, and nothing else.
  • Sign-in codes: when you ask for a code, we email it to you. Codes expire and are used once.
  • Polls and quizzes: we count answers. To stop repeat votes we keep a one-way scrambled value made from your connection and the poll, using a secret that changes every day, so it cannot be traced back to you later. We do not store IP addresses.
  • Comments, if you leave any, are shown with your name.
  • Basic server logs (pages requested, errors) are kept for a short time to keep the site running.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no selling or renting of your data.
  • Sharing buttons are plain links. They load nothing from other companies until you click one.
  • The Google sign-in button loads Google's script only when you open the Join box.

Who handles it

GenXLinked runs on Ghost, an open-source publishing system, on a server we operate. Emails are sent through Cloudflare. If you also follow us on Substack, Substack's own privacy policy applies there.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or delete your account from your account page. To get a copy of your data or have it deleted, write to arun@genxlinked.com and we will do it within 30 days.
