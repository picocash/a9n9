Somewhere between our first passport and our first reading glasses, a lot of us started keeping a list. Not always written down. Just a running sense of the places we'd regret not seeing.

"Travel and new experiences are high on the bucket lists of over-55s post-pandemic," Louise Eaton-Terry, later life lead at Royal London, said in 2021, when the company [published its own survey](https://www.royallondon.com/media/press-releases/press-releases-2021/april/life-begins-at-fifty-five-new-study-reveals-how-the-over-55s-are-planning-to-live-life-to-the-fullest-in-a-post-pandemic-world/). That's the generation just ahead of us. For Gen X itself, we couldn't find a current survey, in any of our eight countries, that asks where we most want to go.

Here is what the older surveys found.

In Royal London's 2021 survey of 1,000 UK adults aged 55 and over, seeing the Northern Lights came first, named by 53%. A trip on the Orient Express came second, at 42%.

In the United States, AARP surveyed slightly more than 1,600 adults who had taken a leisure trip in the past two years, in [December 2016](https://www.travelmarketreport.com/consumer-media/articles/more-than-half-of-baby-boomers-have-a-bucket-list-aarp-says). The baby boomers' top dream within the US was Hawaii, named by 18%; abroad, it was Australia, at 13%. That survey did split out Gen X, but the two reports we read, in [Travel Market Report](https://www.travelmarketreport.com/consumer-media/articles/more-than-half-of-baby-boomers-have-a-bucket-list-aarp-says) and [TIME](https://time.com/4726565/bucket-list-travel-aarp-hawaii/), give different figures for it, so we've left them out.

So the Northern Lights and Hawaii are the over-55s' answers, from 2021 and 2016. We'd like ours.

## Your turn

Tell us the one place you want to see before you turn 60. Not a list: one. Then tell us why that one. Maybe it's somewhere a parent always meant to go, or a city from a song, or a beach you saw on a postcard in 1987.

Tell us, too, the year you were born and the country and city you live in now. We cover the US, the UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and the Philippines, and we'd especially like to hear from Manila and Singapore, where we found no bucket-list survey at all.

From your replies we'll publish the first Gen X bucket list across all eight countries: where we want to go, and whether someone in Manchester dreams of the same place as someone in Manila.

**Name the one place you want to see before 60, and why. Tell us the year you were born, and your country and city.**
