**How to read the ranking:** This is a judgment about application friction first, then technical-audience fit and the ability to reach cash-out within 90 days - not a forecast of income. The top positions favor direct programs over two-stage network applications. Under 100 followers does not by itself disqualify a website-led application, but an existing domain is not the same as an established content audience.

**Important definitions:** "Unconfirmed" does **not** mean bots are allowed. It means the cited public material does not expressly authorize an autonomous affiliate account or specify an AI-authorship disclosure rule. Visybl should be the legal publisher wherever companies are accepted, with an authorized human handling contracts, tax details and identity checks. "Not published" below means no exact public requirement or SLA was established, not that the requirement is zero or waived.

## Synthesis: Access, Conversion And Cash-Out Are Different Gates

Railway and Vultr have the simplest documented account-led paths; Firecrawl adds a short stated review and a particularly strong fit with developer content. Trezor and Ledger fit the self-custody audience, but Trezor publishes an actual rate/window while Ledger leaves material economics to onboarding. DigitalOcean has clearer attribution and a relatively low network payout floor, whereas Apify offers larger recurring percentages but adds a three-customer cash-out condition. [7][9][12][13][22][52][36][35]

The exchange programs have a different conversion mechanism: revenue depends on eligible customers actually trading and generating fees. Password managers and VPNs are easier to connect to a security story, but Proton/Nord's payout gates and Nord's content approval can delay cash. Amazon has a website route around the small social audience, but it still needs original content and qualifying purchases; it is not an immediate workaround for having little distribution. [31][49][25][41][15]

**My recommendation:** Treat the first 90 days as a narrowly focused technical-publishing test, not a follower-growth bet. Publish a real deployment or agent-payment integration, use only relevant links, and track three separate milestones: merchant acceptance, a valid paid conversion, and money actually withdrawable. No defensible dollar forecast follows from having fewer than 100 followers without traffic, conversion and customer-spend data.
